# Interview — DBA (Marta K.), 2026-09-22, 45 min

**Platform.** PostgreSQL 17 on `packdwh-pg01` (on-premises, 2 TB, 16 cores), extensions `pg_cron` and
`postgres_fdw`. Nightly window 01:00–05:30 and it is full: the inventory snapshot (DWH-401) and the
SAP landing fight for it. The hardware lease ends 2027-03, which is the real deadline for the move.

**What is hot.** `mfg.productionrun`, `mfg.machineevent`, `mfg.energyreading`, `sales.orderline`,
`sales.invoice`, `supply.inventorysnapshot`, `quality.inspection`, `quality.labtestresult` take almost
all reads (see `exports/table_rowcounts.csv`). `mfg.packagingproduct`, `mfg.material` and `supply.supplier`
are small and read by every join.

**What is cold.** `sales.pricelist` rows older than two years are never read; `supply.shipment`
before 2024 only for the sustainability extract once a year.

**Pitfalls.** The duplicate `productkey 7` (DWH-412) breaks any merge that assumes a key. Text compares
case-sensitively everywhere, so `quality.inspection.batchcode` keeps `B1234a` and `B1234A` apart (two
batches, on purpose) — but `sales.customer.contactemail` holds case variants of one address that the
application treats as one (R-KEY-1). `startedat`, `receivedat`, `approvedat` and friends are `timestamptz`:
stored in UTC, and the plant's own offset is gone — the Power BI model re-applies `mfg.plant.timezone`
and nobody remembers exactly where. Money columns are `numeric(19,4)`, four decimals.

**Identity keys.** `sales.customer` and `supply.supplier` use `GENERATED BY DEFAULT AS IDENTITY`;
the SAP loads depend on the generated values being stable. Invoices and inventory snapshots take
their keys from the sequences `sales.seq_invoice` and `supply.seq_snapshot`.

**Security.** One read-only role per consumer. For the migration a role `velox_reader`
(CONNECT, USAGE and SELECT on the four schemas) was created on the test copy; see `config/connections.ini`.

**Her ask.** "Move the four hot fact tables first and prove the OEE and receivables numbers match
to the cent before anyone touches the Power BI models."

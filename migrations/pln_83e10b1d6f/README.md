# Migration: BillOfMaterial, EnergyReading, Machine, MachineEvent, MaintenanceWorkOrder, Material, PackagingProduct, Plant, ProductionLine, ProductionRun → velox_dev.mfg

> Generated by Velox from plan `pln_83e10b1d6f`, version 1, approved by hieunle@kms-technology.com on 2026-10-27. Each approved version gets a folder of its own, and the versions before it move to `archive_plans/`: change the plan in Velox, not these files.

## In short

This plan copies the 10 manufacturing tables from packdwh_src.mfg into velox_dev.mfg as Delta tables with the same names in lower case, and lets velox-users read them. The rule-based tests check row counts, columns, NOT NULL, sums and date ranges against the source. The added tests check business rules: dates and quantities in the right order, hours within a day, and machines, lines, plants, materials and products that exist in their parent tables. Before approving, look at the timestamp risks: some columns lose their time zone offset, and some are compared with columns that never had one.

_Written by databricks-claude-sonnet-5-5 from the tables' metadata; its business rules are the tests in the business_rules category._

## Risks

- **MEDIUM** · MachineEvent: OccurredAt (datetimeoffset) becomes a TIMESTAMP in UTC: the point in time is kept, its offset is not.
- **MEDIUM** · MaintenanceWorkOrder: ReportedAt (datetimeoffset) becomes a TIMESTAMP in UTC: the point in time is kept, its offset is not.
- **MEDIUM** · PackagingProduct: PackagingProduct has no primary key: duplicate rows cannot be detected.
- **MEDIUM** · ProductionRun: StartedAt (datetimeoffset) becomes a TIMESTAMP in UTC: the point in time is kept, its offset is not.
- **MEDIUM** · MaintenanceWorkOrder: ReportedAt is converted to UTC while StartedAt and CompletedAt are datetime2 without an offset, so comparing them may be skewed by the original time zone.
- **MEDIUM** · ProductionRun: The links to machine, line, plant and product are plain integer columns that the copy does not enforce, so orphan rows can appear unnoticed later.
- **MEDIUM** · Machine: In the source data already, “Warranty does not end before the machine was installed” does not hold: a finding about the data, not a test of the migration.
- **MEDIUM** · PackagingProduct: In the source data already, “A product is not discontinued before it was launched” does not hold: a finding about the data, not a test of the migration.
- **LOW** · BillOfMaterial: BillOfMaterial has no primary key in the source: the model chose (BomLineKey), unique and never null in the source; the unique-key test checks the copy.
